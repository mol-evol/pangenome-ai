Engineering a living system today means guessing which genes will tolerate each other, then spending years and millions of pounds finding out. Roughly seven in ten complex modifications fail.

Four billion years of evolution has already run that experiment, across millions of genomes. Our Large Pangenome Models read that record and turn it into predictions: which combinations hold, which collapse, and why. Backed by a BBSRC Pioneers grant and a PNAS paper showing pangenome evolution is predictable.
